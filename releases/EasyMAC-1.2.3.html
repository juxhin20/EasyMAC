EasyMAC 1.2.3

  • Hide the strip with one click — a small arrow in the strip's corner tucks it away; a slim arrow stays at the screen edge to bring it back (⌥⇧S works too).
  • Instant desktop switching — swiping between desktops updates the strip as soon as the swipe ends, without a delay and without the strip jumping during the gesture. Thumbnails of other desktops are kept, so they appear immediately.
  • Snappier strip animations.
