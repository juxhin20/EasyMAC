EasyMAC 1.3.3

  • Clicking the notch opens the Tray on every desktop: when the Tray had been left open on another desktop, clicking the notch closed that hidden list instead, so nothing appeared. Now it brings the Tray to the desktop you're on. The Tray, floating folder windows and the notch click area also stay on all desktops when you switch.
  • Remove an item from the Tray in one click: hover a Tray row and a small bin appears on the right. It removes the item from the Tray only; the file stays on your Mac.
