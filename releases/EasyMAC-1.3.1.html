EasyMAC 1.3.1

  • Keep Awake: your Mac now sleeps when the timer ends with the lid closed — in “Lid closed” mode, if the time ran out while the lid was already shut, the Mac could stay awake for hours and drain the battery. EasyMAC now puts it to sleep as soon as Keep Awake ends. (A Mac used closed with an external display and the charger stays awake, as macOS intends.)
