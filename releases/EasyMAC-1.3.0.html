EasyMAC 1.3.0

  • Paste into folders — copy files anywhere (Finder, Mail, EasyMAC) and press ⌘V in a folder list to paste them there, or ⌘⌥V to move them. Right-click a file or empty space for Paste. Files with the same name are kept side by side ("doc 2.pdf"), never overwritten. ⌘C copies the selected files, and pasting into the Tray adds them to it.
  • Strip tab on every screen — when the strip is tucked away, its arrow tab now shows on all your displays, and clicking it opens the strip on that screen.
