EasyMAC 1.2.0

  • Files & Tray — Downloads, Desktop and a Tray in the strip or the menu bar. Drag files straight into Chrome, WhatsApp, Mail. Multi-select, Space for Quick Look, float any list on top of all apps.
  • Clipboard history — ⌥⇧V or the icon: a bar of everything you copied (text, links, images, files). Kept while EasyMAC runs (even across updates) or up to 30 days.
  • Notch HUD — volume, brightness and mute shown right in the notch.
  • Keep Awake — / zzz in the menu bar: 10 min to indefinitely; Display, System only or Lid closed (optional one-time permission, no more password prompts). A small ring next to the notch shows the time left.
  • Mouse — give your mouse wheel its own direction (Standard / Natural) while the trackpad keeps its own.
  • Smoother and lighter: input handling on its own thread (no lag), lighter animations.
  • Options to hide the EasyMAC menu bar icon and the strip footer.
