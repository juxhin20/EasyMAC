EasyMAC 1.0.0

  • First release — compact strip of live window thumbnails with hover preview, pinning and auto-hide.
  • Workspaces — save your apps and window positions per project, restore with one click or ⌥1…⌥9.
  • Snap wheel — hold fn and move the mouse to snap windows to halves, quarters or maximize.
  • Layouts & hotkeys — 50/50, 60/40, thirds, main + stack, grid; ⌃⌥ arrows; ⌥Tab switcher.
  • Starts at login, updates itself, signed and notarized by Apple.
