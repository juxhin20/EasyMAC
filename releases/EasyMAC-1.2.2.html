EasyMAC 1.2.2

  • App icons mode — show windows in the strip as compact app icons instead of live previews (Settings → Sidebar → Windows shown as). Lighter: no screen capture at all.
  • Choose what the strip shows — turn Workspaces, Tray, Folders, Desktops and the footer on or off. Hiding Tray or Folders from the strip keeps them in the menu bar and the notch.
  • Brightness never goes dark — the brightness keys stop at 10% by default (adjustable), so the screen can't go black.
  • Step size for volume & brightness — 1%, 2%, 3%, 5%, 6% or 10% per key press; hold ⌥⇧ for 1% steps.
