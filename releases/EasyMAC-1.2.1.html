EasyMAC 1.2.1

  • Drop on the notch — drag files to the notch: left half sends them with AirDrop, right half keeps them in the Tray. Click the notch to open the Tray.
  • Folders inside lists — double-click a folder to open it in place, ‹ or ⌘↑ to go back.
  • Tray stays open — the file list stays on top of every app until you close it.
  • New Settings tabs — Files & Tray, Clipboard and Keep Awake each have their own page, plus one place to choose which menu bar icons you see.
  • Keep Awake — optional one-time permission so “Lid closed” never asks for your password.
  • Zero lag — permission checks and Spaces queries moved off the main thread; menus and scrolling stay smooth.
  • Fixes: mouse wheel direction with smooth-scrolling mice, menu bar icons hiding together, clipboard history kept across updates, private file permissions for saved clipboard images.
