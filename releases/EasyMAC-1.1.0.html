EasyMAC 1.1.0

  • Desktops row — small numbered desktops (Spaces) at the bottom of the strip. Click to switch, double-click to see all windows of that desktop. Turn on in Settings → Sidebar.
  • Instant desktop switching — one click to enable direct ⌃1…⌃9 jumps.
  • Fullscreen apps — the strip tucks away over fullscreen apps and slides in at the screen edge.
  • Lighter on battery — smarter thumbnail refresh (up to every 10 minutes), paused while the display sleeps.
  • Option to hide the “EasyMAC ” footer.
  • Fixes: snapping EasyMAC's own window, windows keep clear of the strip on every display, Workspaces settings layout.
